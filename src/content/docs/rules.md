# Rules & Prizes — Agents in the Wild

The short version: build something that perceives, decides and acts in the
physical world, do the work inside the two weeks, submit a report and a video by
the deadline. Here it is in full — if anything is still unclear, ask the team.

---

## The rules

1. **Build window: 13 Jun → 28 Jun.** You're judged on what you achieve during
   the sprint. Existing projects are welcome — but only progress made inside the
   two weeks counts.

2. **It must touch the physical world.** Your system should perceive something
   in its real environment, make a decision nobody pre-programmed, and take an
   action that matters. We'll accept submissions that don't quite break earth,
   but expect the winning projects to be deployed, not demoed on a laptop.

3. **Solo or team — both fine.** Find collaborators at kickoff if you want
   them. One submission per project.

4. **Any stack.** Cloud inference, hybrid, existing frameworks like OpenClaw —
   all allowed. Local-first is encouraged: it preserves privacy and works where
   there's no internet.

5. **Submit by end of 28 Jun.** See [Submission](#submission) below. No
   submission, no judging.

6. **Judging is four criteria, equally weighted (25% each):** Innovation,
   Impact, Progress, Deployment. The top five present live at **Demo Night,
   7 Jul at LocalGlobe** — live demos preferred.

7. **Loan kit comes back.** The Raspberry Pis (and any other loaned equipment)
   must be returned at the end of the two weeks — easiest at Demo Night.
   **[Sign up now →](https://luma.com/awkhqorw)**

8. **Be sensible in the wild.** You're deploying real systems in real places —
   respect privacy, get permission for the spaces you deploy in, and build in
   appropriate guardrails.

---

## Submission

Due by **end of Sunday 28 Jun**. One submission per project, containing:

1. **A report** — a doc or slides covering three things: the **scope** of the
   project, its **purpose**, and **what you actually achieved** in the two
   weeks. Short and honest beats long and polished — what broke and what you
   learned counts as achievement.
2. **A video** — show the **build process** and the **agent in action** in the
   wild. Phone footage is fine; we care about the deployment, not the
   production values.

**How to submit:** put both in one **Google Doc** — the report, plus links to
GitHub, the demo video (YouTube is fine) and anything live — and paste the doc
link on **[your team page →](/agents-in-the-wild/team)**. That's also where you
form your team and describe your project — do that at kickoff, not on deadline
day.

The judges pick the **top five** from the submissions to present live at
**[Demo Night, 7 Jul at LocalGlobe →](https://luma.com/awkhqorw)**. We'll
contact the top five before the night so you can prepare — live demos preferred,
video fallback accepted.
